## Everything is Digital
- "Situate the electronic digital comptuer within a larger history of information technology" to understand the history of computing better
- Software can be found in small devices in the from of micropocessors, and in computers, so "the skills required to design and program digital devices are therefore the same as those to design and program more conventional computers"
- Suggests using the term "digitization" over "computerization" to be more all incompassing of information technologies
	- "They all, by definition, operate on data stored in a digital format"
	- Uses digital to mean a way of representing information that unifies modern technologies
	- Is not the same thing as quantification, "the principal goal of digitization is manipulation"
		- "These numbers [MP3 file of audio] are not so much a measurement of sound as a model of sound ... [the digitized version] is valuable because it is easy to capture, store, communicate, analyze, and transform"
	- "The combination of data and the means of manipulating it"
	- "The integration of digital data and digital model allows for the digital simulation of the original physical system"
